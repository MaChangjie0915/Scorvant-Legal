隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日 · Effective September 29, 2026

Scorvant 是一款网球记分与比赛记录工具。本政策说明 Scorvant 如何处理您在 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上使用 App 时产生的信息。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 比赛与球员信息：球员名称、比赛设置、比分、时间线、规则模板和偏好设置主要保存在您的设备上。
  • 健康与健身信息：在您授权后，Apple Watch 可向 Apple 健康写入网球训练，iPhone 可读取与比赛有关的心率、能量和训练摘要。详细健康数据保留在您的设备与 Apple 健康中，不会由 Scorvant 上传到开发者服务器，也不会通过 Scorvant 的 CloudKit 同步。
  • iCloud 同步：启用相应 Pro 功能时，比赛、球员资料、模板及部分 App 设置可通过与您 Apple ID 关联的私有 CloudKit 数据库同步。开发者不会获得您完整的 Apple ID 登录凭据。
  • 购买信息：订阅和永久购买由 Apple 处理。Scorvant 仅接收确认产品与使用权限所需的购买状态，不接收您的完整银行卡或支付账户信息。
  • 支持通信：当您发送支持邮件时，我们会收到您主动提供的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，并仅将其用于处理请求与必要的后续沟通。

2. 我们不会做的事情

Scorvant 不使用第三方广告 SDK，不进行跨 App 或网站追踪，不出售您的个人信息，也不将 HealthKit 数据用于广告、营销或数据经纪。

3. 存储、同步与安全

本地数据受 iOS 和 watchOS 的设备安全机制保护。iCloud 数据由 Apple 的 CloudKit 服务处理并存储在您的私有数据库中。互联网传输和云服务不存在绝对安全保证，但 Scorvant 仅使用完成所选功能所需的系统服务。

4. 保留与删除

您可以在 Scorvant 的“设置 → 删除所有数据”中删除本地比赛、球员、模板和设备内健康详情，并请求删除 Scorvant 同步到 iCloud 的 App 数据。Apple 健康中的训练记录需要在“健康”App 中管理。购买历史、退款和订阅记录由 Apple 按其政策处理。

5. 您的选择

您可以在系统设置中撤销健康数据权限、关闭 iCloud，或停止使用同步功能。撤销权限不会自动删除此前已经保存的数据。

6. 儿童隐私

Scorvant 不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不包含社交发布或定向广告功能。如您认为儿童向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

7. 政策变更与联系

功能或法律要求发生变化时，本政策可能更新，并在本页面标明新的生效日期。如有隐私问题或删除请求，请发送邮件至 scorvant@163.com。

Privacy Policy

Scorvant is a tennis scoring and match-recording app. This policy explains how Scorvant handles information created when you use the app on iPhone and Apple Watch.

1. Information we process

  • Match and player information: Player names, match settings, scores, timelines, rule templates, and preferences are primarily stored on your devices.
  • Health and fitness information: With your permission, Apple Watch can save tennis workouts to Apple Health, and iPhone can read match-related heart-rate, energy, and workout summaries. Detailed health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. Scorvant does not upload it to developer-operated servers or sync it through CloudKit.
  • iCloud sync: When an eligible Pro feature is enabled, match data, player profiles, templates, and selected app preferences can sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID. The developer does not receive your full Apple ID sign-in credentials.
  • Purchase information: Apple processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases. Scorvant receives the product and entitlement status needed to unlock features, but not your complete bank-card or payment-account details.
  • Support communications: If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them only to answer and follow up on your request.

2. What we do not do

Scorvant does not use third-party advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage.

3. Storage, sync, and security

Local data is protected by iOS and watchOS device security. Apple processes iCloud data in the private CloudKit database associated with your account. No internet transmission or cloud service can be guaranteed absolutely secure, but Scorvant uses system services only as needed for the features you select.

4. Retention and deletion

Use “Settings → Delete All Data” in Scorvant to remove local matches, players, templates, and on-device health details and to request deletion of synced Scorvant iCloud data. Manage Apple Health workouts separately in the Health app. Apple handles purchase history, refunds, and subscription records under its own policies.

5. Your choices

You can revoke Health permissions, turn off iCloud, or stop using sync in system settings. Revoking permission does not automatically delete information already stored.

6. Children’s privacy

Scorvant is not designed to collect children’s personal information and does not include social publishing or targeted advertising. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to support.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change and will post a revised effective date here. Questions and privacy requests: scorvant@163.com.

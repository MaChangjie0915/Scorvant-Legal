Technical Support

联系我们

遇到问题或希望提出建议，请发送邮件至 scorvant@163.com。

建议在邮件中写明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、Scorvant 版本以及问题发生的步骤。请不要发送不必要的健康数据或支付信息。

常见问题

为什么购买页面一直显示正在连接 App Store？

请确认设备已联网、已登录可用于购买的 Apple ID，并稍后重试。TestFlight、沙盒和正式 App Store 的商品状态可能需要一段时间同步。

如何管理或取消年度订阅？

打开 iPhone“设置”，轻点您的 Apple ID，然后选择“订阅”。订阅、续订、取消和退款均由 Apple 处理。

已经购买，为什么 Pro 没有解锁？

在 Scorvant Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”。请确保当前设备使用的是完成购买时的 Apple ID。

如何删除数据？

打开 Scorvant“设置 → 删除所有数据”。这会删除本地 App 数据并请求删除已同步的 Scorvant iCloud 数据。Apple 健康中的训练记录需要在“健康”App 中单独管理。

我的健康数据会上传吗？

比赛健康详情保留在设备和 Apple 健康中，不会上传到开发者服务器，也不会通过 Scorvant 的 CloudKit 同步。详情请阅读隐私政策。

Support in English

For help or feedback, email scorvant@163.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Scorvant version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send unnecessary health or payment information.

Why does the purchase screen keep connecting to the App Store?

Confirm that the device is online and signed in to an Apple ID that can make purchases, then try again later. TestFlight, sandbox, and production App Store product status can take time to update.

How do I manage or cancel the annual subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, select your Apple ID, and choose Subscriptions. Apple handles subscription renewal, cancellation, and refund requests.

I purchased Pro. Why is it still locked?

Choose Restore Purchases on the Scorvant Pro screen and make sure the device is using the Apple ID that completed the purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Open “Scorvant → Settings → Delete All Data.” This removes local app data and requests deletion of synced Scorvant iCloud data. Manage Apple Health workouts separately in the Health app.

Is my health data uploaded?

Detailed match health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. It is not uploaded to developer-operated servers or synced through Scorvant’s CloudKit records. See the Privacy Policy.
